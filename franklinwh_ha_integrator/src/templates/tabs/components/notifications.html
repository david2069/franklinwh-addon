Seeing extra buttons FHAI didn't send? (e.g. Snooze 5/15/60 min)

FHAI sends a specific button set per action type — HOLD prompts get Hold State / Override; charge/export prompts get Approve / Skip. Any additional buttons on your phone (Snooze, Remind Me, etc.) are added client-side by the Home Assistant Companion app on your device — not by FHAI or your HA server. They ride along regardless of what FHAI sends.

To manage them (per device):

  1. Open the Home Assistant Companion app on your phone
  2. Settings (sidebar) → Companion App → Notifications → Actions (or Categories depending on version)
  3. Delete any custom actions you don't want (e.g. Snooze 5 mins / 15 min / 1 hour)
  4. On iOS 26+: also check iOS Settings → Notifications → Home Assistant → Time Sensitive which enables the OS-level "Remind Me" gestures

Any button whose action code doesn't start with FWH_ is phone-side. FHAI's callback endpoint only reacts to FWH_* actions.
