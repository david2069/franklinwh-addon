Engine Diagnostics

Read-only view of the SmartDispatch decision chain, LP optimiser output, and engine config. Data refreshes every 15 s.

How to read this page

Every 30 seconds the SmartDispatch engine decides what your battery should do (HOLD, GRID_CHARGE, GRID_EXPORT). Many sources can want different things — your Strategy Matrix rules, time schedules, Automation Builder rules, and the cost-minimising LP/greedy optimiser. This page shows exactly which one won this tick and why.

Steps run in strict order (top of the chain below wins first): your explicit rules always beat the optimiser. If a matrix rule matches your live prices + SoC, the optimiser's recommendation is superseded. The optimiser is a smart fallback — it only speaks when nothing else has an opinion.

Read the sections top-to-bottom: 1) what won right now, 2) the 6-step ladder (winning step highlighted green), 3) what the optimiser would have done (shown even when it didn't win), 4) your active config, 5) all Strategy Matrix rules. Everything is read from tables the engine writes to — this page does not run the engine or change anything.

About the Decision Chain

The SD engine runs a fixed 6-step precedence chain on every 30 s Micro tick. The winner from the first non-silent step becomes the tick's dispatch decision.

The LP / greedy optimiser at step 4 always runs (its output feeds into the context regardless), but only "wins" when steps 1–3 have no match. This means your explicit user rules always override the optimiser — the optimiser is a smart fallback, not an override authority.
