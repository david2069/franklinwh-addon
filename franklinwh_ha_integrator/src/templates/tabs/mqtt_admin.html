Old / Duplicate Devices

Home Assistant keys a device on its identifiers, not its name. When ours changed, the old identifier stopped being published to — and because discovery messages are retained, Home Assistant keeps rebuilding the old device, with every entity it ever had, on each restart. Those are the duplicates that show up in entity pickers. Removing them here clears the retained message that recreates them.

Breaking Change — Entity IDs Will Change

Changing the entity prefix will orphan existing HA dashboards, automations, history, and template sensors that reference the old entity IDs. This cannot be undone automatically.
