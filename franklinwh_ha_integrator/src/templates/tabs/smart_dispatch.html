Key concept: The engine is a real-time complementary override layer, not a 24h schedule builder. It evaluates pricing every 5 minutes and temporarily overrides your gateway with a direct command (like GRID_CHARGE or STANDBY). When no pricing condition is met, the engine issues no command — your gateway simply continues operating in its configured native mode (TOU, Self-Consumption, or Emergency Backup) without interruption.
